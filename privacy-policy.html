Prompt Studio is provided by [DEVELOPER NAME]. Version 1 helps you assemble prompt text from bundled templates and your answers. It does not require an account.

Information in the app

Your saved prompts, answers associated with them, recent prompt history, and preferences are stored on your device. Prompt Studio does not send this content to a developer-operated server. The current app contains no advertising, analytics SDKs, tracking SDKs, or in-app purchases. It does not request access to your photos, contacts, microphone, or location.

You choose what to share

Copying a prompt places it on your system clipboard, which is governed by your device settings. Using the system share sheet sends content to the destination you select. Opening a recommended AI tool launches an external service. Those providers process information under their own policies; Prompt Studio does not automatically submit your prompt or reference photos to them. If you choose to upload a reference photo in an external AI tool, that upload takes place outside Prompt Studio.

Storage and removal

Saved prompts remain locally until you remove them or delete the app. Recent history is limited to the latest 200 entries. The app offers controls for removing saved items and history. Device or cloud backups may include app data according to your operating-system settings. Deleting the app removes its local data, but managing existing backups is handled through your device or backup provider.
